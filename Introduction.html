🔍 What is a Market Basket Recommendation Engine?

A Market Basket Recommendation Engine analyzes customer purchasing behavior to identify product associations. It determines which items are often bought together and recommends them to customers at the right time—either while shopping or just before checkout. Think: Customers who bought a phone also added a screen protector and a case. We show those recommendations automatically, increasing your chances of a larger sale.
